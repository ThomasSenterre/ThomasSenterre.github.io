About me

I am a PhD candidate in labour economics at the Université libre de Bruxelles (CEBRIG and DULBEA) and the Université de Mons (Soci&ter), supervised by François Rycx and Mélanie Volral. My research lies at the intersection of labour, education and migration economics. Using Belgian matched employer-employee data, I study the labour market integration of immigrants and their descendants, with a focus on wage gaps, the role of fields of study and education-occupation mismatch.
